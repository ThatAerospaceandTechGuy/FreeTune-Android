Features

A music player that gets out of the way.

Everything you need to listen, nothing you didn't ask for.

Ad-free streaming

Play what YouTube has, straight from the source, without ads or interruptions between songs.

Real offline downloads

Pick a quality, download a song or a whole playlist, and every playlist gets its own folder.

Synced lyrics

Timed lines highlight as the song plays. Lyrics are fetched automatically and saved with your downloads.

Queue, repeat, sleep timer

See every upcoming song, jump anywhere in the queue, loop one or all, and stop after 15–60 minutes.

Install

Three taps and you're listening.

FreeTune is sideloaded — it is not distributed through Google Play.

  1. 01

    Download the APK

    Grab the latest build above ({{SIZE}}). Any version works; newer builds include the bug fixes.

  2. 02

    Allow the install

    Android asks permission to install from your browser or file manager. That warning is normal for sideloaded apps.

FAQ

The honest answers.

Is FreeTune on Google Play?

No. FreeTune is distributed as a signed APK from this site and GitHub Releases. Play Store policy and the app's extraction model don't mix, so sideloading is the only supported path.

Is downloading from YouTube legal?

FreeTune is an unofficial client. Downloading audio may violate YouTube's Terms of Service and copyright law depending on the content and where you live. Keep downloads for personal use and respect the rights of the people who made the music.

Does it need Google Play Services?

No. FreeTune runs on plain Android 8.0+ and does not use Play Services, Firebase or any Google SDK.

Why does Android warn me about the APK?

Because it wasn't installed from the Play Store. Builds are signed with a consistent release key, so updates install cleanly over older versions. You can verify any download against the SHA-256 shown above.

Where do downloads go?

Into the app-specific Music folder, Android/data/com.freetune.app/files/Music/FreeTune/, with a subfolder per playlist. Other players may not index that folder, but FreeTune plays everything in it offline.

Will updating wipe my library?

No. Installing a newer APK keeps your playlists, likes, history and downloads. Uninstalling the app removes its data, so update instead of reinstalling.

Where do lyrics come from?

From LRCLIB, a free and open lyrics database. FreeTune looks songs up by artist, title and duration, caches the result, and saves a matching .lrc beside every download. If a song isn't there, you can pair your own .lrc file in the player.

Does it work offline?

Downloaded songs play fully offline, lyrics included. Streaming, autoplay and online lyrics need a connection; autoplay simply stops at the end of your downloaded queue.
